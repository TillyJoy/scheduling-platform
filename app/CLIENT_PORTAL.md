# Client Portal and Self-Scheduling

The client portal provides secure, limited access for clients to schedule, reschedule, or cancel appointments.

Clients must only have access to their own scheduling information.

## Scheduling Link

Clients may receive a secure scheduling link by:

- SMS
- Email

The link should be unique to the scheduling request.

The client should not be required to create a full application account for basic scheduling unless the organization chooses to require authentication.

## Security

Scheduling links must use secure, non-guessable tokens.

Links should:

- Expire according to configurable rules
- Be revocable
- Be invalidated when appropriate
- Avoid exposing internal database IDs
- Provide only the minimum necessary client information

## Available Services

The client should only see services that are currently eligible and available to them.

The client should not see:

- Ineligible services
- Internal eligibility notes
- Internal funding details unless explicitly configured
- Internal job statuses
- Internal Auditor notes
- Other clients
- Other appointments

## Property and Unit Selection

If a property contains multiple units, the client may be presented with only the units relevant to their scheduling request.

The system must support:

- Entire property scheduling
- Selected-unit scheduling
- Single-unit scheduling

The organization may configure which option the client is allowed to select.

## Appointment Options

The client receives appointment options generated by the same Scheduling Engine used by Schedulers.

Available appointments must account for:

- Service eligibility
- Service duration
- Auditor qualifications
- Auditor availability
- Zone
- Travel requirements
- Contractor requirements
- Existing appointments
- Scheduling holds
- Organization rules

## Auditor Selection

Clients should not normally select a specific Auditor unless the organization explicitly enables that feature.

The Scheduling Engine should assign an appropriate Auditor automatically.

## Appointment Confirmation

After successful scheduling, the client should receive a confirmation.

The confirmation may include:

- Appointment date
- Appointment time
- Property address
- Unit information when appropriate
- Services scheduled
- Rescheduling link
- Cancellation link
- Contact information for the organization

## Reminders

The organization may configure automatic reminders.

Supported channels may include:

- SMS
- Email
- Push notification where applicable

Reminder timing must be configurable.

## Rescheduling

The client may use the original secure link to reschedule when permitted.

The system must:

1. Verify the scheduling request.
2. Recalculate available times.
3. Release the original appointment only after a replacement appointment is successfully selected, where practical.
4. Create the new appointment.
5. Preserve the original appointment history.
6. Notify affected staff.
7. Synchronize the change with external systems.

## Cancellation

The client may cancel when permitted.

The system should optionally ask for a reason.

Possible reasons may include:

- Schedule conflict
- No longer interested
- Unable to attend
- Transportation issue
- Other

The client may optionally provide additional information.

After cancellation:

- Auditor availability is released
- Contractor availability is released when applicable
- Schedulers are notified
- The appointment history is preserved
- External systems are updated when configured

## Client Notes

Clients may optionally provide additional information when:

- Scheduling
- Rescheduling
- Cancelling

Client-provided information should be clearly identified as client-submitted information.

## Late Auditor Notification

If an Auditor reports that they are running late, the system may automatically notify the client according to organization settings.

The message should provide only necessary information.

Example:

"Your Auditor is running approximately 15 minutes late. We apologize for the delay."

## Auditor Cancellation

If an Auditor cancels or becomes unavailable, the client may receive an automatic notification.

The organization may instead require a Scheduler to contact the client.

## No-Show

A client no-show is managed by the Scheduling Platform's scheduling workflow.

The client should not automatically be removed from the program.

The appointment may be changed to:

`Needs Rescheduling`

The organization may configure the number of attempts before a service is considered declined.

## Client Privacy

Client-facing screens must use minimum necessary information.

Clients must never be able to access:

- Other clients
- Internal notes
- Internal staff information beyond what is necessary
- Internal funding information unless explicitly authorized
- Internal audit information
- API credentials
- System configuration

## Accessibility

The client portal should support:

- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Desktop browsers
- Screen readers where practical
- Keyboard navigation
- Large text
- Clear language
- High-contrast accessibility options

## Language

The system should be designed so additional languages can be added without redesigning the scheduling engine.

## Communication Preferences

Organizations may configure:

- Email only
- SMS only
- Email and SMS
- Client opt-in requirements
- Reminder frequency
- Notification templates

## Failed Messages

If an email or SMS cannot be delivered:

1. The failure must be recorded.
2. The Scheduler may be notified.
3. The system should not silently mark the client as contacted.
4. Retry behavior should be configurable.

## Communication History

The system should maintain a communication history showing:

- Date/time
- Communication type
- Recipient
- Template used
- Delivery status
- Failure information when applicable

The actual message content should be retained according to organizational data-retention policies.
